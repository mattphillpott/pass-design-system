FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Pass Revise really matched to my exam board?
Yes. Every note, question and past paper is tagged to a specific qualification, subject and board (AQA, Edexcel, OCR, WJEC Eduqas, CIE), so you only ever see content for the exam you’re actually sitting.
What can I use for free?
You can read a large amount of revision content and attempt exam questions for free. Smart Mark feedback, full note libraries and past-paper downloads are part of Premium.
How does Smart Mark work?
You answer in your own words and Smart Mark compares your response to the mark scheme, awarding the points it can clearly identify. When it isn’t confident, it tells you and offers a self-assessment checklist so you’re never misled.
Who is Pass Revise for?
Students revising for GCSE, IGCSE, AS and A-Level. You need to be 13 or over to create an account.
